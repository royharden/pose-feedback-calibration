## Proposed explanation for adult volunteers

The proposed study measures counting of ordinary squat cycles and refusal behavior from local video. It does not grade form, depth, appearance, tempo, health, injury risk or exercise safety, and is not coaching or medical advice. Participation would involve 12 clips of at least 32 seconds each: six front/45-degree/side squat clips with ordinary or brief obstruction in front of the lens, and six scripted non-squat activities (standing/talking, crossing the view twice, sitting/standing once, picking up an object, waving and exiting/re-entering). People choose their own ordinary pace; no unusual/unsafe action or coaching is requested. Study-specific acceptability and actual willingness have not been established.

Proposed processing uses the exact named approved pretrained runtime/model, yet to be completed below, operated locally by the authorized human under the approved offline procedure. AI agents orchestrate software preparation; coding-model context receives only bounded redacted aggregates/commitments, never raw video, real coordinates, individual labels or consent/name mappings. Recording, trace and label data can be personal/biometric-adjacent; they are not presumed anonymous. Native offline observation would concern outbound transport during that run, not a vendor telemetry certification.

Proposed storage is an operator-supplied encrypted local private root outside every sync/Git tree, with access limited to approved study roles. Actual root, access roles, contact and procedures require review. Public real video/images/skeleton traces/coordinate exports and individual labels are not permitted by this draft.
